# Module 7 - Attacks and Defences

## 1. CIA Triad

The **CIA Triad** is the foundation of information security.

### Confidentiality
- Protects data from unauthorized access.
- Examples: Encryption, Passwords, MFA.

### Integrity
- Ensures data is accurate and unchanged.
- Examples: Hashing, Checksums, Digital Signatures.

### Availability
- Ensures systems and data are accessible when needed.
- Examples: Backups, Redundancy, Disaster Recovery.

## 2. Cryptography

### What is Cryptography?
The practice of securing information by converting it into an unreadable format.

### Key Terms
- Plaintext
- Ciphertext
- Encryption
- Decryption
- Key

### Types of Encryption
- Symmetric Encryption (One Key)
- Asymmetric Encryption (Public & Private Keys)

### Common Algorithms
- AES
- RSA
- SHA (Hashing)
